## October 12 investigation decisions

The final engineering pass repairs measured ingestion/synthesis failures and preserves the 48-case reviewed-content parity. It does not certify fresh generation, unseen-company financial correctness or release readiness. Backend expansion stops when no measured independently executable gap remains; reviewer/holdout access and paid capture authorization remain external inputs.

[Langfuse masking](https://langfuse.com/docs/observability/features/masking) and [Phoenix tracing](https://arize.com/docs/phoenix/tracing/concepts-tracing/how-does-tracing-work) support OpenTelemetry diagnostics, but a new collector/retention stack is not justified for current single-owner usage. Keep local pinned exports, coverage/attempts and request-scoped usage/failure receipts. Revisit only when a concrete investigation needs cross-request spans and private-content retention/access is approved.

UI references reviewed October 12: [Perplexity Finance/Spaces](https://www.perplexity.ai/changelog/what-we-shipped-june-27th), [Elicit source quotes and review tables](https://elicit.com/solutions/systematic-review), [AlphaSense workspace](https://www.alpha-sense.com/platform/), [Vercel Chatbot](https://github.com/vercel/chatbot), [assistant-ui](https://github.com/assistant-ui/assistant-ui) and [shadcn accessible panels](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/components/base/resizable). Adopt visible source context, quote inspection and structured research results. Streamlit reuses the current trusted-local runtime and contracts; a React/Next.js client offers finer panel/routing control but adds a second server, API/credential wiring and two application toolchains. The owner subsequently requested a design checkpoint and decision before major UI implementation. Frontend direction is pending; the provisional Streamlit draft is archived locally and no major UI is integrated. A dedicated React/Next.js research workspace is being evaluated on final interaction/portfolio quality, not framework novelty. Neither choice requires a chat SDK, gateway or new AI functionality.
